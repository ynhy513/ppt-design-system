# Multi-page visual storyboarding

**English** | [简体中文](visual-storyboard.md)

Turn reference language into a related sequence of pages. Inputs may be a reference or `STYLE.md`, target medium and known narrative roles/page constraints. In visual-only work, plan tasks such as establishing a theme, comparing two items or showing detail with image/title/chart placeholders. Without confirmed copy, label roles as visual hypotheses and size the sequence accordingly.

## 1. Shared rules and allowed variation

State the visual direction and its identity in one sentence. Choose 2–4 deck-wide anchors, such as alignment axes, type hierarchy, background/accent roles or photo light/crops. Record local rules under their layouts.

| Decision | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Inheritance | Concrete relationship, scope and source page/region, such as shared top edges and a central gap for peer exhibits. Prefer recurring evidence. One-page observations become extensions when applied elsewhere. |
| Cross-page extension | New page task, retained anchors, adjustable parameters and reasons. Focus scale/position, image/text ratio, density, crop and color area may change by task; check adjacent-page continuity. |
| Unresolved | Decision-relevant unknowns, such as estimated screenshot fonts or absent dense-page samples, with provisional placeholders/ranges. |

Use evidence labels **measured / observed / inferred / extended / unresolved** and record constraint strength separately. User-approved brand rules may be hard constraints; a measured source value may only be one page type's default. For components/assets, record both attributes and purpose: a fine square frame groups peers; a side-lit close-up reveals texture. Choose new assets by equivalent roles.

## 2. Match composition and elements to page roles

Begin with what readers must understand, then find source compositions serving the same task. Decide entry focus, comparison versus sequential reading and evidence area before selecting details. Cite source page/region per candidate and use [composition rules](page-composition.en.md) for alignment, offset and image variants.

| Reference composition | Page role | Transfer checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Large hero, short title, generous space | Opening/theme/mood | One focal point, title placement and topic-bearing imagery. |
| Equal columns, shared axes, symmetric dividers/frames | Peer comparison | Common dimension, weight and order. Unequal columns need clear hierarchy and an extension record. |
| Offset close-up, narrow text/image column, annotations | Detail/evidence/mechanism | Expose key crop details, place notes nearby and subordinate explanation area to focus. |
| Ordered list, numbering, hierarchical boxes/table | Process/steps/evidence | Preserve order/grouping; use axes for sequence, rows/columns for peers. Reserve data/chart/explanation slots first. |
| Low-density field or recurring motif | Transition/pause/closing | Density/light changes signal sections; reduce/restate the opening motif and preserve final focus space. |

When moving from DOC/PDF, distinguish continuous-reading headers, paragraphs and tables from visual heroes, columns and hierarchy. Reorganize reading units for the target: preserve column axes or split long tables into standalone evidence pages. For PPT, distinguish masters, fixed decoration and page-specific composition. When the medium changes, preserve hierarchy/order/alignment before coordinates and dimensions.

For example, a shared top edge on two source images can transfer to peer comparisons. A gray-green accent expanded to a full chapter field is an extension: explain the transition role and limit it through existing space/color roles.

## 3. Compose deck rhythm

1. Plan roles before pages. Identify overall impression, detail, comparison, pause and return-to-overview moments. Detail pages can reuse overview colors/numbers/contours; recap restores the same cue.
2. Separate narrative importance, density and visual strength; assign first focus per page and inspect thumbnails. An overview meant to be remembered must outweigh later decorative fields. Dense explanation may need a quiet page afterward. Vary adjacent focus size/position, space, light/dark or symmetry deliberately while retaining shared anchors.
3. Use one storyboard table recording order/role; focus/composition/slots; inherited rules/sources; extensions/reasons; continuity and strength relative to the preceding page. Inspect the whole sequence first, then role/layout fit per page. If previews are requested, review same-scale thumbnails.

Storyboard acceptance requires clear tasks/focus, traceable inheritance, bounded extensions and repetition/variation serving transitions/reading order. A text storyboard is planning evidence; rendering and final visual quality require separate verification.

For another model/assets, bind overview/detail/callbacks to the same motif and lock reviewed rhythm/candidates through the [reproduction contract](reproducibility.en.md).
